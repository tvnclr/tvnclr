## About

I work in technical support and operations for local businesses in São Paulo: point-of-sale systems, receipt printers, card terminals and small-office networking.

Outside of that I build and modify tools for problems I run into myself, mostly on Android (rooted devices) and Windows, and I'm studying Computer Science at FIAP, focused on data science, AI and platform engineering.

The repositories below are the ones with my own work in them. The rest of the list are forks I keep for reference.
